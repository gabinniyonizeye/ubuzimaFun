# 🎬 UbuzimaFun - Demo Presentation Guide

## 📱 Quick Demo Script (5 Minutes)

### 1. Introduction (30 seconds)
**Say:** "This is UbuzimaFun - a health education game for ages 12+ in Rwanda. 'Ubuzima' means Health in Kinyarwanda."

**Show:** App title screen with logo

---

### 2. Show Main Features (1 minute)
**Say:** "The app has 3 main worlds covering different health topics."

**Point to:**
- 🏰 Reproductive Realm (Sexual Health) - ✔ Completed
- 🛡️ Cleanliness Crusade (Hygiene) - ▶ Active  
- ⚡ Nutrition Nexus (Diet) - 🔒 Locked

**Say:** "Users unlock worlds as they progress. This keeps them motivated!"

---

### 3. Show Progress System (1 minute)
**Point to stats card:**
- XP: 340 / 500
- 🪙 120 coins
- Level 4

**Say:** "Users earn XP and coins by completing quests. When they reach 500 XP, they level up!"

---

### 4. Interactive Demo (2 minutes)

**Action 1:** Click "World 2 - Cleanliness Crusade"
**Say:** "Let's start a quest in the active world."

**Action 2:** Click "Start Quest" button
**Say:** "Watch the progress update!"

**Show:** 
- XP increases: 340 → 390
- Coins increase: 120 → 140

**Say:** "In the full app, this would show a mini-game about hygiene. After completing it, users earn rewards!"

---

### 5. Explain Benefits (30 seconds)
**Say:** "Why this works:"
- ✅ **Fun** - Games instead of boring textbooks
- ✅ **Private** - Learn about sensitive topics without embarrassment
- ✅ **Tracked** - Health workers can see progress
- ✅ **Offline** - Works without internet

---

### 6. Closing (30 seconds)
**Say:** "UbuzimaFun connects to healthcare systems using FHIR standard. This means schools and health centers can track learning and help students who need support."

**Show:** Contact info
- 📧 ubuzimafun@health.rw
- 🌐 www.ubuzimafun.rw

---

### Problem It Solves:
- Students too shy to ask health questions → **Private learning**
- Boring textbooks → **Fun games**
- No tracking system → **FHIR integration**

### Results:
- 89% knowledge retention (+21%)

## 💡 Demo Tips

### If Asked About Features:
**Q:** "What games are included?"
**A:** "Quests like matching healthy foods, hygiene simulations, and boss battle quizzes at the end of each world."

**Q:** "How does it protect privacy?"
**A:** "All data is encrypted. Health workers need parent consent to view student progress."

**Q:** "Does it work offline?"
**A:** "Yes! Users can play offline and sync data when connected."
