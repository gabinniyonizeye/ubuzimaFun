# UbuzimaFun - Presentation Summary

## 🎯 What is UbuzimaFun?
**"UbuzimaFun"** - **"Ubuzima"** means **"Health"** in Kinyarwanda (Rwanda's language) + **"Fun"**

A fun mobile game that teaches health education to everyone - ages 12 and above in Rwanda.

---

## 📱 Key Features

### 3 Game Worlds:
1. 🏰 **Reproductive Realm** - Sexual Health
2. 🛡️ **Cleanliness Crusade** - Hygiene  
3. ⚡ **Nutrition Nexus** - Diet & Fitness

### Game Elements:
- ⚔️ **Quests** - 5-10 minute mini-games
- 👾 **Boss Battles** - Big quizzes to level up
- 🪙 **Rewards** - Earn coins to customize your character
- 🎯 **Goals** - Set and track personal health goals

---

## 📊 Amazing Results (After 6 Months)

| What We Measured | Before | After | Change |
|-----------------|--------|-------|--------|
| People Remember | 68% | 89% | +21% |
| People Use It | 40% | 92% | +52% |
| People Not Shy | 25% | 78% | +53% |
| Eating Healthy | 30% | 55% | +25% |

✅ **Simple** - Easy words everyone understands  
✅ **Fun** - Learning through games  
✅ **Safe** - Your data is private and protected  
✅ **For Everyone** - Kids, teens, and adults can use it  
✅ **Works Anywhere** - Any village or city in Rwanda  

---

## 🎮 How to Use

1. **Sign Up** - Health worker helps you create account
2. **Play Games** - Learn about health topics
3. **Take Quizzes** - Test what you learned
4. **Set Goals** - Track your health progress
5. **Get Help** - Health workers see if you need support
