# 🎮 Interactive Game Demo Guide

## 🎯 What Is This?

An **interactive hygiene quiz game** that runs INSIDE your presentation! You can play it live during your presentation to show how UbuzimaFun works.

---

## 🚀 How to Use During Presentation

### Step 1: Navigate to Slide 3 (Solution Slide)
- Use arrow keys to go to the "How It Works" slide
- You'll see the 3 game worlds

### Step 2: Click the Demo Button
- Look for the **"🎮 Play Demo"** button under "⚔️ Quests"
- Click it to launch the interactive game

### Step 3: Play the Game Live!
- Answer 5 hygiene questions
- Show how the game works in real-time
- Audience can see the scoring system

### Step 4: Close the Demo
- Click the **X** button in top-right corner
- Or click outside the game window
- Continue with your presentation

---

## 🎮 Game Features

### What the Audience Will See:

1. **Timer** - 60 seconds countdown
2. **Score** - Points earned (20 per correct answer)
3. **Progress** - Questions answered (X/5)
4. **Questions** - Real health education content
5. **Feedback** - Instant correct/wrong answers
6. **Rewards** - XP and coins at the end

### Sample Questions:
- "How often should you wash your hands?"
- "How long should you brush your teeth?"
- "How often should you shower or bathe?"
- "What should you do when you cough or sneeze?"
- "How often should you change your underwear?"

---

## 💡 Presentation Tips

### When to Use the Demo:

**Best Time:** During Slide 3 when explaining "Quests"

**What to Say:**
1. "Let me show you how the game actually works"
2. Click the demo button
3. "This is a hygiene quiz - one of our mini-games"
4. Answer 1-2 questions live
5. "See how it gives instant feedback?"
6. "Students earn points and learn at the same time!"

### Demo Flow (2-3 minutes):

```
1. Click "Play Demo" button
   ↓
2. Read first question aloud
   ↓
3. Click an answer (correct or wrong)
   ↓
4. Show the feedback message
   ↓
5. Answer 1-2 more questions
   ↓
6. Close demo or finish all 5
   ↓
7. Show final score screen
   ↓
8. Close and continue presentation
```

## 🔥 Pro Tips

### Make It Interactive:
- **Ask the audience** which answer they think is correct
- **Let them vote** before you click
